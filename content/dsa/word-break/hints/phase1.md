## Level 1
Cutting off the longest word that fits, or the shortest, and never looking back can walk into a dead end. Try a short message where the first piece has two candidates.

## Level 2
Whether the rest of the message can be split depends only on where the rest begins, not on which pieces came before it. How many different places can the rest begin?

## Level 3
Work out, for every position from the front, whether the message up to that position can be split.
