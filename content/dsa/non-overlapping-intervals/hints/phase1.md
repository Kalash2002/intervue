## Level 1
Cancelling the fewest requests is the same as keeping the most. Rules such as "cancel the longest request" or "keep the request that starts first" sound right; try each one on Example 1 and Example 2 before you trust it.

## Level 2
Look at the request that ends first, of all of them. Is there ever a reason not to keep it?

## Level 3
In any set of requests you could keep, swapping its earliest-ending request for the one that ends first overall never creates an overlap.
