## Level 1
Walk the list once. At each node, which way should its link point when you are done?

## Level 2
As soon as you change a node's `next`, you lose the way to the rest of the list. What do you have to hold on to before you change it?

## Level 3
Keep the part you have already reversed as a list of its own, and move the nodes over to its front one at a time.
