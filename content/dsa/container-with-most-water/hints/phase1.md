## Level 1
Trying every pair of walls works. What do you give up on when the two walls get closer together, and what could make up for it?

## Level 2
Start with the widest tank, the two outer walls. To get a bigger volume with a smaller width, the level must go up. Which of the two walls limits the level, and what happens if you move the other one?

## Level 3
Once you have measured a tank, one of its two walls can never be part of a better one. Decide which, and why.
