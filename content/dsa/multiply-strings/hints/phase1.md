## Level 1
Adding `num1` to itself `num2` times is correct but can take 10^200 rounds. How do you multiply two long numbers on paper, and what does that need besides the multiplication table?

## Level 2
On paper you write one shifted row per digit of `num2` and add the rows. Where in the final answer does the product of digit `i` of `num1` and digit `j` of `num2` end up?

## Level 3
Numbered from the left in an array of m + n digits, that product always lands at position i + j + 1, so the rows are not needed.
