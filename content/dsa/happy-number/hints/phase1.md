## Level 1
Follow the chain by hand from a start that is not happy, such as 85. It never reaches 1, so a loop that runs until it sees 1 never ends. What does happen to the chain, and how could your loop notice it?

## Level 2
Every number has exactly one next number, so once a value comes up a second time, the chain repeats from there forever. How can you tell that a value has come up before, either by remembering the ones you saw or without remembering them?

## Level 3
Run two copies of the chain, one a step at a time and one two steps at a time: on a loop the fast one catches up with the slow one.
