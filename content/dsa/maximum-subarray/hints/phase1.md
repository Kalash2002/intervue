## Level 1
Adding up every run works, but at 100,000 days it is billions of sums. Cutting the ledger at every losing day is no shortcut: a small loss between two big gains belongs in the best run.

## Level 2
Every run that ends on a given day is a run that ends on the day before, with this day added, or this day on its own. Which of those can be the best, and what do you need to remember from the day before to choose?

## Level 3
A run before today whose total is negative can only drag today down.
