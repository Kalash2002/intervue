## Level 1
Fix the day you sell. What is the best day to have bought on, given that you can only look at days before it?

## Level 2
Trying every pair of days works but is slow. For a given selling day, only one thing about the earlier days matters. Could you carry it along as you walk through the prices once?

## Level 3
For each selling day only the cheapest earlier price matters. Think about why the highest and lowest prices over the whole list are not the right pair.
