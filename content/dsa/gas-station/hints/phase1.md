## Level 1
Starting at the stop with the most charge, or the one that gains the most on its own leg, can still run out further round. Driving the loop from every stop works but costs up to n laps. Ask what one failed attempt tells you about the stops it passed.

## Level 2
Suppose a start runs out on the leg from stop i. Every stop between that start and stop i was reached with a battery of zero or more. Could starting at one of them with an empty battery get any further?

## Level 3
If the stops give at least as much charge in total as the legs use, some stop works.
