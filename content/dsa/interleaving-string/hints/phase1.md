## Level 1
Taking each card from whichever pile shows it breaks down when both piles show the same card. In Example 1 both offer an l for the first card and an e for the fifth, and each time only one of the two choices leads anywhere.

## Level 2
Once some cards of the deck are accounted for, the order in which they came from the piles no longer matters, only how many came from each: i cards from `left` and j from `right` means the next card of the deck is the one at i + j.

## Level 3
Whether the first i cards of one pile and the first j of the other can make the first i + j cards of the deck depends only on the same question with one card fewer, from one pile or the other.
