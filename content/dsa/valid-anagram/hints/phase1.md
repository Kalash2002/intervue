## Level 1
If two words are anagrams, what do they have in common even though the letters sit in different places? Think about what is the same and what is different.

## Level 2
Only how many times each letter appears matters, not where. Can you record those numbers for one word and compare them with the other? Also decide what the lengths tell you before you count anything.

## Level 3
Keep a table of counts indexed by letter. Add for one word and subtract for the other, and think about which signal says "no" as early as possible.
