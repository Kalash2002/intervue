## Level 1
Listing the routes one by one works on a small floor and never finishes on a large one. Count a 2 by 4 and a 3 by 4 floor by hand and look at where the parcel can be one move before the goal.

## Level 2
Every route into a cell arrives either from the cell above it or from the cell to its left, and no route does both. How many routes reach a cell, then, if you know the counts for those two?

## Level 3
A cell's count depends only on counts for cells above it and to its left, so the counts can be filled in an order where each one is ready when it is needed.
