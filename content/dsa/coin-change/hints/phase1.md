## Level 1
Paying with the largest coin that fits is not always fewest, and it can even miss every way to pay. Try a few small amounts by hand.

## Level 2
Whatever coin you hand over first, the rest must also be paid with as few coins as possible. How many different rests can there be?

## Level 3
The fewest coins for each total from 0 up to the amount can be worked out from the fewest coins for smaller totals.
