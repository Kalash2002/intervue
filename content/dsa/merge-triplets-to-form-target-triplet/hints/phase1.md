## Level 1
Overlaying every preset can overshoot, and finding each target level somewhere in its channel is not enough either, because the preset that has it may bring a level that is too high elsewhere. Trying every set of presets is correct but doubles with each preset. Ask which presets could ever be part of the mix.

## Level 2
An overlay never lowers a level. What does that mean for a preset that is above the target in even one channel? And what can a preset that is at or below the target in every channel do to a mix?

## Level 3
You never have to choose among the presets that fit: one mix of them decides the answer.
