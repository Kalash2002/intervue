## Level 1
There are three kinds of group in which a digit may not repeat. Name them, and ask how many groups of each kind the board has.

## Level 2
For every cell holding a digit, you want to know quickly whether the same digit was already seen in its row, its column or its box. What can you keep for each group to answer that?

## Level 3
Keep one record of seen digits per row, per column and per box. The row and column of a cell are known, so work out which formula turns the pair (row, column) into a box number from 0 to 8.
