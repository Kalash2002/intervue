## Level 1
Which courses can a student take on the very first day? Once those are done, which courses become possible next?

## Level 2
Keep, for each course, how many of its prerequisites are still unfinished. When a course is taken, which of those numbers change, and how do you find the next course to take without scanning every course again?

## Level 3
When no course is left with zero unfinished prerequisites, every course still waiting sits on or behind a loop.
