## Level 1
Pick one bar and imagine its height as the height of the rectangle. How far to the left and to the right can that rectangle stretch before it meets a shorter bar?

## Level 2
For every bar you need the nearest shorter bar on each side. Searching outwards from each bar can be slow on a flat or staircase skyline. Is there a way to find those boundaries while walking the bars once?

## Level 3
Keep the bars that are still waiting for a shorter bar on their right, in order.
