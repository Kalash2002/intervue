## Level 1
Asking only whether `word` can be found in `text` gives 1 for Example 1, and so does counting where it appears with its letters side by side. Each choice of a t and a later o before the e and the s is a way of its own.

## Level 2
Look at the first letter of the text. Every way either strikes it out, or keeps it as the first letter of the word when the two match. Both leave the same kind of question about shorter strings, and the answer is the sum of the two.

## Level 3
Every question that comes up is about the text from some position on and the word from some position on, so there are only about as many different questions as there are pairs of positions.
