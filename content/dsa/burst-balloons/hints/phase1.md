## Level 1
Try two rules of thumb on Example 1: burst the smallest balloon first, or always burst the one that pays most right now. Neither reaches 133, because each burst changes who is next to whom.

## Level 2
Splitting the row at the first balloon burst does not work: its two neighbours become neighbours of each other, so the sides still meet. Ask instead which balloon is burst last, and what it stood between while the others went.

## Level 3
The questions that come up are all of one kind: the most coins from the balloons strictly between two balloons that are still standing, with a 1 at each end of the row for the missing neighbours.
