# Grader notes: Meeting Rooms III

Tests cover: the sample, a single request, a single room, a delayed request that runs in the room that frees first, the lowest-numbered free room being chosen when several are free (the answer is room 1 and not 0), a tie between rooms 1 and 2 that goes to 1, requests given out of start order, and a case with two rooms and sessions up to a billion minutes long where delayed finishing times pass the `int` range (an `int` solution picks the wrong room). The large case has 80,000 rooms and 100,000 requests starting at minutes 0 to 99,999 with lengths 50,000 to 149,999, so most rooms are busy and many requests wait; the expected rooms are 203 for 80,000 rooms (3 sessions, 37 rooms tie), 14 for 70,000 rooms (3 sessions, 351 tie) and 4 for 60,000 rooms (3 sessions, 1,445 tie); all were found with an independent heap simulation and agree with the naive solution.

The naive solution scans every room for each request. That is O(requests times rooms), so on the large test it exceeds the limit and 3x the limit. The reference keeps a heap of free rooms and a heap of busy rooms.
