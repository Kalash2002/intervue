# Grader notes: Decode Ways

Tests cover: the sample; a 0 that cannot stand alone (`"00"`, `"0123"`, `"9001"`, `"130"`, all 0); a 0 joining the 1 or 2 before it (`"10"`, `"20"`, `"1020"`, `"110"`, all 1; treating 0 as a letter of its own gives more); pairs above 26 (`"27"` gives 1, `"2626"` 4, `"2829"` and `"99"` 1; accepting every pair that starts with 2 fails here); pairs from 17 to 19 (`"17"` gives 2, `"819"` 2; accepting only pairs whose second digit is at most 6 fails here and on the sample); pairs that start with 0 (`"105"` and `"1203"`, 1 each; reading `05` or `03` as a letter gives 2 and 3, and also fails `"0123"` and the sample); chains where every neighbour pair is a letter (`"1212"` gives 5, `"22222"` 8); 200 seeded random strings of 1 to 14 digits, drawn mostly from 0, 1 and 2, checked against a search in the test over every set of cut points; and two strings of 100 digits: 22 copies of `21`, a `2` and 55 nines, whose 45-digit chain reads like the Fibonacci numbers, F(46) = 1,836,311,903 ways (the nines pair with nothing), and 49 copies of `12` followed by `50`, which has no reading at all (0).

In the second long test the count for a prefix passes the `int` range long before the final `0` brings it back to 0; with `int` addition wrapping around exactly, any correct pass still returns 0, which the statement's guarantee covers. A solution that checks for overflow with `Math.addExact` would fail it.

The two long tests have a limit of 200 ms each (3x is 600 ms). The reference, two running counts, finishes each in a few milliseconds. The naive solution is the plain recursion "one digit or two, then the rest"; its calls grow by a factor of about 1.6 per digit, so it is still running after 20 seconds on both, far past the limit and 3x. Its recursion is at most 100 deep, so it times out rather than overflowing the stack. A plain recursion that first rejects any 0 not preceded by 1 or 2 returns the dead-end string at once, but still times out on the first long test. The walkthrough's memoised recursion is O(n) like the reference and passes.
