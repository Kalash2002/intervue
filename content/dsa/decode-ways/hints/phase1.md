## Level 1
Read the message from the front: its first letter was written with either one digit or two. Watch what a 0 allows.

## Level 2
However the first few digits were read, the number of ways to read the rest is the same. What does that let you avoid working out again?

## Level 3
The number of ways to read the first i digits depends only on the numbers for the first i - 1 and the first i - 2 digits.
