## Level 1
A square with sides along the axes is fixed by the query corner and one more corner. Which other corner would you pick, and how many ways are there once all four corners are known, when each can have several copies?

## Level 2
Trying every stored point as the opposite corner works, but most stored points are nowhere near the query's diagonals. Is there a corner you could start from that only a few stored points can be?

## Level 3
A stored point in the query's own column fixes the side length, and then the square lies in one of just two columns.
