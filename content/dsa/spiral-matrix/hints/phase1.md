## Level 1
Walking with a direction and turning at the edge works, but to turn in time you must know which tiles are already cleaned. Is there a way to know when to turn without remembering every tile?

## Level 2
Count the tiles on each straight stretch of the walk in Example 1. How do the counts change from one stretch to the next?

## Level 3
After each stretch, the next stretch along the same axis, horizontal or vertical, is one tile shorter, because one row or column has just been used up.
