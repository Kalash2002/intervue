## Level 1
The median depends only on the one or two numbers in the middle. Sorting everything on every question repeats almost all of its work. What do you really need to know about the numbers below the middle, and the ones above it?

## Level 2
Picture the numbers split into a lower half and an upper half of equal size, or with the lower half one larger. The median is read from the largest of the lower half and the smallest of the upper half. What does a new number do to that split?

## Level 3
Each half only ever needs its extreme value at hand, the one nearest the middle, while numbers keep arriving.
