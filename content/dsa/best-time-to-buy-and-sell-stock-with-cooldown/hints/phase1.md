## Level 1
Buying before every rise and selling after it would be best without the rest day, but the rest day can block the next cheap buy. Work Example 1 by hand and see which rise has to go.

## Level 2
At the end of any day the desk is in one of three situations: holding a share, having just sold (so tomorrow is a rest day), or holding nothing and free to buy. What is the best profit it can have reached in each one?

## Level 3
Each situation's best profit at the end of a day follows from the three best profits at the end of the day before.
