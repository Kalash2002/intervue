# Grader notes: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock with Cooldown

The rules are the classic ones: up to 5,000 days, prices from 0 to 1,000, one share at a time, one action a day, no buy on the day after a sale. The answer is a single number, so no tie rule is needed. The largest possible profit is 1,667 trades of 1,000, which fits an `int`.

Tests cover: the sample (`[6,2,9,4,8,3,12]` 16, `[2,7,6,11]` 9, a flat price 0); two back-to-back rises the rest day keeps apart (`[1,4,2,7]` 6, not 8); selling a day before the peak so the rest day lands on it (`[11,2,0,3,11,0,9]` 12, where holding from 0 to 11 gives 11); keeping the share through several dips (`[1,5,4,8,7,11]` 10); prices of 0 and a sale on the last day (`[0,1000]` 1,000, `[4,0,0,7]` 7); 300 seeded random runs of 1 to 9 days with prices 0 to 20, checked against a search in the test over every choice of buy, sell or nothing for each day; and two runs of 5,000 days. The first repeats 0, 1000, 500: a trade and its rest day take three days, so at most 1,667 trades fit and each earns at most 1,000, and buying every 0 reaches 1,667,000. The second repeats 1000, 0: a trade earns 1,000 only from a 0 to the next 1000, after its rest day the next 0 is three days on, so a 0 can be bought at most every four days, and the 1,250 trades from days 1, 5, 9 and so on make 1,250,000.

Traps, each checked with a scratch solution: summing every rise (no rest day) fails the sample, the back-to-back test, the before-the-peak test, the dips test, the random test and the zigzag; updating `idle` before buying from it, which lets the desk buy on the rest day, fails the same six; starting `hold` at 0, a free share, fails six including the random test (`[19,14,1]` gives 19); a two-day rest fails the before-the-peak test, the random test and the repeating rise.

The two long runs have a limit of 200 ms each (3x is 600 ms). The reference, three running values, finishes each in a few milliseconds. The naive solution is the walkthrough's brute force, which tries every move from every day; it is still running after 4 s on both, past the limit and 3x. Its recursion is at most 5,000 deep, which fits in the default stack, so it times out rather than overflowing it. The walkthrough's Better, the memoised search, is O(n) like the reference and passes; its recursion is also at most 5,000 deep.
