## Level 1
Play the minutes out by hand on Example 1. Which oranges can possibly turn in the next minute, and do you need to look at the whole crate to find them?

## Level 2
Only an orange that turned in the previous minute can spoil something new. If you kept those oranges in a list, what would one minute of spreading cost, and how do you stop an orange that turns now from spreading in the same minute?

## Level 3
Start every mouldy orange in the same queue at minute 0 and process the queue one minute's worth at a time.
