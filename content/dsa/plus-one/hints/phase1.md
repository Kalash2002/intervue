## Level 1
Turning the digits into a `long`, adding one and turning it back works for short numbers, but a `long` stops at 19 digits and the number can have 100. How do you add one on paper?

## Level 2
On paper you start at the last digit. When does adding one change more than that digit, and how far to the left can the change go?

## Level 3
A 9 is the only digit that passes the carry on to its left.
