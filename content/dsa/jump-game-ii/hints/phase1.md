## Level 1
Hopping as far as you can each time is not always fewest, and neither is hopping to the station with the largest range. Trying every route is correct but explodes. Think about which stations the message can have reached after one hop, after two hops, and so on.

## Level 2
The stations the message can reach within a given number of hops form one unbroken stretch starting at station 0, because a hop may always be shorter. How do you get the stretch for one hop more from the current one?

## Level 3
The next stretch ends at the farthest point that any station in the current stretch can reach.
