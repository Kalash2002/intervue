## Level 1
Starting a straight at whatever card comes first can use up a card that a lower straight needed, and cutting the sorted hand into blocks of `size` breaks when copies of one number land in the same block. Trying every grouping is hopeless at this size. Look for one card whose place you can be sure of.

## Level 2
Think about the smallest card in the hand. Which position in its straight can it hold, and what does that say about the rest of that straight?

## Level 3
Keep a count per number instead of the cards themselves: then all the straights that start at one number can be taken in a single step.
