## Level 1
Add the way you would on paper, one column at a time. The lists already give you the columns in the order you need them.

## Level 2
A column's result can be 10 or more. What do you carry into the next column, and what happens when one list runs out before the other?

## Level 3
The loop is not over when both lists are empty: a carry that is still left is one more digit.
