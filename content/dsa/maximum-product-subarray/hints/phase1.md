## Level 1
Multiplying out every run works, but at 100,000 stages it repeats a great deal of work. Ask what the best run that ends at a given stage looks like, and what a negative gain does to it.

## Level 2
A run ending at this stage is either this stage alone or a run ending at the stage before, extended by one. With sums the largest run before would be the one to extend; with products a negative gain turns the most negative product into the largest one.

## Level 3
Carry the smallest product of a run ending at each stage as well as the largest.
