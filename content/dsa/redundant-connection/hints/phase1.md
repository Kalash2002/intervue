## Level 1
Imagine laying the cables one at a time in the order they are listed. Is there a moment when a new cable joins two buildings that can already reach each other?

## Level 2
That moment comes exactly once, and the cable laid then closes the loop. Because the loop is complete only when its last listed cable arrives, that cable is the answer. What you need is a fast way to ask "are these two buildings already connected?" while cables keep arriving.

## Level 3
Keep the buildings in groups that merge as cables arrive, where each group is named by one representative building.
