## Level 1
Ignore which group is which. At any one minute, what single number decides whether the shuttle is fine?

## Level 2
That number is the total of the groups aboard at that minute, and it changes only when a group boards or leaves. How could you visit just those minutes, in order?

## Level 3
Each request gives two moments at which the number aboard changes. Put all of those moments in one sorted order and keep a running total as you pass them. Decide carefully what should happen first when a group leaves and another boards at the same minute.
