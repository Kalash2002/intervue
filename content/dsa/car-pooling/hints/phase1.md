## Level 1
Ignore which group is which. At any one minute, what single number decides whether the shuttle is fine?

## Level 2
That number is the total of the groups aboard at that minute, and it changes only when a group boards or leaves. How could you visit just those minutes, in order?

## Level 3
When one group leaves at the very minute another boards, the two changes share a minute. Decide which should be applied first before you compare the total with the seats, and why.
