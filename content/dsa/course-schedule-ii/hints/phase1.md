## Level 1
Which courses could go first in the order? After writing those down, which courses are now allowed to follow?

## Level 2
Track, for each course, how many of its prerequisites are not yet in the order. Taking a course changes some of those numbers; which courses should you look at, and when does a course join the order?

## Level 3
The order in which courses become free to take is itself the answer, as long as every course becomes free.
