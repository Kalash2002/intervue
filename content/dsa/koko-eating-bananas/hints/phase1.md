## Level 1
For one chosen rate, can you work out how many hours the whole queue takes? What happens to that number as the rate goes up?

## Level 2
Faster never takes longer, so the rates split into a run that misses the deadline followed by a run that meets it. You are looking for the first rate of the second run, and the largest job's size is always in it.

## Level 3
Count the hours for a rate in a type that can hold the total of a slow rate over many large jobs.
