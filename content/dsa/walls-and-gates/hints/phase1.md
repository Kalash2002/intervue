## Level 1
For one room you could search outwards until you meet an exit. How much of that work is repeated when the next room over does the same search?

## Level 2
Turn the question round: instead of each room looking for an exit, let the exits reach out to the rooms. In what order must rooms be reached so that the first distance written into a room is already the smallest?

## Level 3
Start the search from every exit at once, so rooms are reached in order of their distance to the nearest exit.
