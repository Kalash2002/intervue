# Grader notes: Walls and Gates

Each test compares the whole plan after the call, with the marker printed as INF. Tests cover: the sample; rooms no exit can reach keeping `2147483647`, including two unreachable rooms next to each other (code that adds one to the marker before checking it wraps round to a negative number there); a plan with no exit at all; the nearest of two exits winning, in a row and in a column; walls forcing a six-step detour; plans with no open room (exits and walls only, one-cell plans); forty seeded random plans of up to 8 by 8 checked against a separate search from every room in the test; and two 1,000 by 1,000 plans: one corridor of 500,500 rooms winding through the plan with the exit at its start (three cells and the sum of all distances are checked, so a room left unfilled shows up), and an exit in every fourth column (250,000 exits, sum 1,002,000).

Each large test has a limit of 2,000 ms (3x is 6,000 ms). The reference, one breadth-first search from all exits at once, took 23 to 61 ms per large test on a cold JVM. The naive solution searches from every open room until it meets an exit: it timed out on the corridor even with the limit raised fivefold (10,000 ms), and passes the many-exits plan in about 170 to 200 ms, because every search there stops within three steps. The walkthrough's Better, one search per exit, times out on the many-exits plan; a recursive search would overflow the call stack on the corridor.
