## Level 1
Taking every other house is not always best: sometimes the right choice skips two houses in a row. Neither is grabbing the richest house first. Decide one house at a time instead.

## Level 2
For the last house there are two choices: take it, and then the house before it is off limits, or leave it, and then the rest of the street is unchanged. What do you need to know about the shorter streets to compare the two?

## Level 3
The best total for the first i houses depends only on the best totals for the first i - 1 and the first i - 2 houses, so carry those two numbers along the street.
