## Level 1
On a straight street this is House Robber. The circle adds exactly one rule: the first and the last house cannot both be taken.

## Level 2
In any allowed choice, at least one of house 0 and house n - 1 is left alone. If you knew which one, what would the rest of the circle look like?

## Level 3
Removing one house breaks the circle into a straight street, so solve the straight-street problem twice, once without each end, and keep the better answer. Mind the circle of one house.
