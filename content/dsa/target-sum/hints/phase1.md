## Level 1
Trying every choice of signs doubles the work with each number, and 60 numbers have 2^60 choices. Once the first few signs are fixed, what do the remaining signs need to know about them?

## Level 2
Only the running total of the signed numbers so far matters, and it always lies between minus the sum of all the numbers and plus that sum. The sum is at most 1,000, so there are few different running totals.

## Level 3
The numbers given a plus sign form a group, and the target together with the sum of all the numbers fixes exactly what that group must add up to.
