## Level 1
For one day, the answer is found by looking forward until something warmer shows up. What makes this slow if you do it for every day, especially for a long run of falling temperatures?

## Level 2
Think about days that are still waiting for a warmer day. When a new day arrives that is warmer than some of them, which of the waiting days does it answer, and does it matter in what order they are waiting?

## Level 3
Keep a stack of waiting day positions whose temperatures never rise from bottom to top. When a new day is warmer than the top, pop it and record the gap in days; repeat until the top is not colder, then push the new day. Think about why equal temperatures stay on the stack.
