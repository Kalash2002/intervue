## Level 1
Following the water downhill from every cell answers the question, but on a 200 by 200 map that is 40,000 searches, many of them over the same ground. Is there a direction to search in that would serve many cells at once?

## Level 2
Instead of asking where water from a cell ends up, ask which cells can send water to a given ocean. Where would such a search start, and which way would it be allowed to step?

## Level 3
Search backwards from each ocean's edge cells, stepping only to neighbours at least as high; a cell reached from both oceans is an answer.
