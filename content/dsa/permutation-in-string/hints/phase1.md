## Level 1
A rearrangement of the word has the same letters in the same quantities. Which stretches of the text could possibly match, and what do they all have in common?

## Level 2
Every candidate stretch has the same length as the word. Counting the letters of each stretch from scratch repeats a lot of work between stretches that overlap. What changes when you move from one stretch to the next?

## Level 3
Between neighbouring stretches only two letters change: one enters and one leaves. Keep a running summary of how the current stretch compares with the word, and update it by those two letters instead of recomputing it.
