## Level 1
Picking the cheaper of the two steps ahead of you each time can lead you onto an expensive step later. Think instead about how you arrive somewhere.

## Level 2
You can only arrive at a position from one step below or two steps below it. If you knew the cheapest way to arrive at both of those, what would the cheapest way to arrive here be? The landing is a position too.

## Level 3
Each position's cheapest arrival cost depends only on the two positions below it, so compute those costs from the floor upwards and keep just the last two.
