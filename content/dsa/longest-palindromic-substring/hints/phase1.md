## Level 1
Checking every substring works, but much of the work repeats: testing a long candidate covers the shorter one inside it, which also gets a test of its own.

## Level 2
A stretch is a palindrome exactly when its two end characters match and the stretch between them is a palindrome too. What is the smallest stretch every palindrome can be grown from?

## Level 3
Every palindrome grows outward from its middle, and a middle is either one character or the gap between two neighbouring characters.
