## Level 1
Work out where the tile at row r, column c ends up after the turn. Copying every tile to its new place in a fresh grid is easy; the catch is that the result has to end up in the grid you were given.

## Level 2
Moving a tile to its new place in the same grid overwrites the tile that was there. Follow that one: where does it go, and the one after it? How many moves until you are back where you started?

## Level 3
The tiles that trade places come in fours, one on each side of the same ring of the board, so one spare variable is enough to carry them round.
